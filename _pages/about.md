I am a first-generation college sophomore at Washington University in St. Louis with a broad interest in the field of AI safety.

Specifically, I’m interested in how AI models (LLMs for text, VLMs for videos, DMs for images, etc.) can be invisibly watermarked (robustly, without loss of quality) so that the produced content can be reliably distinguished from human-generated content.  I’m also interested in how safety mechanisms for artificial intelligence can be enforced through public policy.

I'm developing with SynthID!
